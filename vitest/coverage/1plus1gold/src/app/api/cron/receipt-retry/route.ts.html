/**
 * GET /api/cron/receipt-retry
 *
 * Retries WhatsApp receipt delivery for completed orders where the initial
 * auto-send failed (typically due to Vercel cold-start timeout on the webhook).
 *
 * Runs every 5 minutes via Vercel Cron.
 *
 * Scope: orders with no receipt_sent_at whose RECEIPT BECAME DUE in the last 48h
 * — i.e. created recently, OR marked paid recently. See the cutoff below for why
 * the second half matters.
