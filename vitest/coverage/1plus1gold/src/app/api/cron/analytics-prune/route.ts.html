/**
 * GET /api/cron/analytics-prune — enforces the 14-month analytics retention
 * (Measurement spec §05/§10). Deletes raw events + session roll-ups older than
 * the window, bounding table growth and privacy exposure (and giving the
 * unauthenticated /api/track ingest a hard upper bound). CRON_SECRET-gated.
 *
 * Deletes in bounded batches so a large backlog never holds a long lock.
 * ai_answer_probes + search_console_daily are weekly/daily-small and not pruned
 * here.
 *
 * Also enforces a short retention on `spx_webhook_captures` — the SPX inbound-
 * webhook debug ledger (raw body + signing headers). That table is a temporary
 * forensic aid, not order state; a 30-day window bounds its growth and shrinks
 * the PII/credential-exposure surface should its service-role RLS ever regress
 * (sec findings 8c20af68 / 91d5725f). Same batched helper, no new plumbing.
 *
 * And enforces retention rule **R6** on `marketing_engagement` — the handles,
 * platform user ids and comment text of members of the public who comment on our
 * posts. They are not customers, there is no AML hold, and the PDPA
 * publicly-available-data exception covers COLLECTING a public comment, not
 * keeping it indefinitely. After 12 months the person is REDACTED in place and
 * the non-personal engagement shell is kept, so past engagement counts do not
 * silently change. See content/governance/pdpa-retention-schedule.html.
 */

/**

 * the row. Nulls the identifying fields and empties the raw webhook payload.
 *
 * `raw_payload` is `NOT NULL`, so it is emptied to `{}` rather than nulled.
 * `platform_event_id` is deliberately kept: it identifies the COMMENT, not the
 * commenter, it is half the table's uniqueness key, and it is what makes
 * re-ingest of the same comment idempotent.
 *
