/**
 * GET /api/gold-yield/dashboard
 * Returns all data needed for the Gold Yield participant dashboard:
 * - agreement status
 * - enrolled bars with per-bar sales
 * - FIFO queue (anonymised)
 * - earnings summary — profit share AND returned principal
 * - payout history
 *
 * ▶ PRINCIPAL IS PART OF WHAT IS OWED (operator decision, 2026-09-19: "regardless
 * of hedge or no hedge path — the participant gets back the principal also").
 * Before this the dashboard reported only the profit share, so a participant
 * whose grams had sold saw a number that was a fraction of what they were due
 * and no sign of their capital coming back at all.
 *
 * It is reported as its OWN line rather than folded into `total_earned_sgd`,
 * which keeps meaning exactly what it always meant. A single blended figure
 * would make it impossible to tell a good month from a bar being liquidated.
 *
 * Hedged and un-hedged differ here and only here: the hedged principal is what
 * they paid, the un-hedged one is their bar revalued at the spot on the day it
 * sold — which can be less. `principal_per_g` is stamped on each sale row by the
 * settlement, so this route sums a recorded fact and never re-derives it.
 */
