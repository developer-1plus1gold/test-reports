/**
 * MAG own-bar / group-pool SET-UP (docs/mag-quote-automation-design.md §2, §4):
 * after the customer accepts and an admin taps Confirm, the bar is set up with no
 * further admin work —
 *
 *   1. the bar's own MAG bar type at the accepted % (checkout then charges exactly
 *      the quote — no change to the pricing kernel);
 *   2. a physical bar of the quoted product from stock (FIFO, with its CoA serial),
 *      listed reserved / group with closing date = today + the duration (decision 2a:
 *      the clock starts at set-up); or, with none in stock, ONE BullionKing purchase
 *      order for it — the request waits as `awaiting_bar` and set-up resumes when the
 *      bar is received (receipt hook + the lifecycle cron);
 *   3. the requester added as member (a group pool also gets its share link,
 *      lib/mag/group-invites.ts);
 *   4. the request closed as `live` and the customer told "your bar is open".
 *
 * RESUMABLE + IDEMPOTENT: each part records what it made on the request (bar type,
 * listing, PO), so a retry continues where it stopped and never makes a second one.
 * A failure is written to `setup_error` and returned; nothing is half-listed
 * without a member, because the member is added before the request goes live and
 * the listing is the request's own (listing_id) for the retry to find.
 *
 * WHY a purchase order, not a consignment obligation (Mukesh asked for "a
 * consignment order, just like we do for a normal sale"): a sale's consignment
 * obligation (consignment_procurements) IS that sale's cost of goods, and the table
 * only accepts an order line or a conversion as its source. A MAG bar's cost is
 * booked gram by gram from the listing as the customer buys — an obligation would
 * book the whole bar as sold on day one and count it twice. So the bar is ordered
 * from BullionKing as owned stock, priced like any BullionKing order (their
 * StoneX-indexed cost + the 2 % card fee folded in), received and paid the usual way.
 */
