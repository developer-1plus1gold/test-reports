/**
 * Consignment PO post-process (Step 6, Design A).
 *
 * For every consignment obligation that doesn't yet have a PO artifact, this
 * creates a consignment purchase order (via the shared createPurchaseOrder),
 * links it to the obligation, and notifies admins + (when possible) the supplier.
 *
 * WHY a drain, not an inline step: PO creation must not constrain sale creation
 * (the customer has already paid), and serverless fire-and-forget is unreliable.
 * So the obligation is written synchronously at sale time (unchanged — it nets
 * stock_qty and is the accrual/COGS source), and this drain — run by a cron —
 * creates the PO afterward. Bounded-latency, never-lost, mirrors the HitPay
 * reconcile pattern.
 *
 * FINANCE: the PO is a parallel ARTIFACT only. The accrued supplier payable and
 * COGS stay sourced from consignment_procurements — this drain writes NOTHING to
 * ledger_entries or the balance sheet. Guards in the PO receipts/payments routes
 * reject is_consignment POs so they can't leak into the owned-stock or cash-basis
 * payable paths.
 *
 * IDEMPOTENT: creates the PO, then claims it with
 * `UPDATE … SET purchase_order_id = ? WHERE id = ? AND purchase_order_id IS NULL`.
 * If the claim matches 0 rows (a concurrent drain won), the just-created PO is
 * compensating-deleted and the row is skipped — so a row can never get two POs.
 */
