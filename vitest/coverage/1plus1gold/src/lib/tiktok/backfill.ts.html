/**
 * TikTok backfill — post existing Drumbeat reels to TikTok, one a day (Mukesh 2026-11-17).
 * Server-only. Design: docs/tiktok-organic-integration.md step 3b.
 *
 * Mukesh's rule: reuse what already went out on Instagram — he approved and posted it.
 * So preparing a piece COPIES, never generates:
 *   - the chosen Instagram caption → an approved TikTok caption (unless TikTok has one)
 *   - the chosen Instagram hashtags → TikTok hashtags (when there are any)
 *   - the Instagram reel (first approved VIDEO on its channel post, else the piece's
 *     newest approved video) + its Reel cover → the TikTok channel post
 *   - `tiktok` appended to target_channels
 * Publishing then goes through the normal publish route (the daily cron calls it), so
 * every Drumbeat gate still applies; a published piece takes TikTok as an ADDED channel.
