/**
 * Canonical site title + description — the SINGLE source for the social-share
 * card and the SEO meta description.
 *
 * ▶ Why this file exists (2026-08-29). `SITE_DESCRIPTION` was previously
 * written out TWICE in `src/app/layout.tsx` — once for `metadata.description`
 * (the SEO meta description) and again for `metadata.openGraph.description`
 * (what WhatsApp / Slack / iMessage / LinkedIn render in a link preview). Two
 * copies of one sentence is a drift hazard: editing one and not the other
 * silently gives search engines and social crawlers different claims about the
 * business, and nothing fails. One constant, imported twice, removes that.
 *
 * ▶ Where the WhatsApp link preview comes from: WhatsApp's crawler fetches the
 * pasted URL and reads the Open Graph tags out of the page `<head>` —
 * `og:title`, `og:description`, `og:image`. Next.js renders those from
 * `metadata.openGraph` below. It is NOT read from the app, the manifest, or
 * any WhatsApp-side setting. WhatsApp caches aggressively, so an updated
 * preview only appears on links shared after the deploy (an already-sent
 * message keeps the old card forever).
 *
 * ▶ Accuracy rules for the description — all three were violated by the text
 * this file replaced, and the first two were reported by Mukesh from a real
 * shared link:
 *   1. We are a GOLD DEALER, not a "split gold dealer". Splits (the Premium
 *      Curve) are one product line; we also sell the full standard-bar range
 *      across Valcambi, PAMP, the Austrian Mint and others, plus Gold Yield,
 *      My Gold Tree and buyback. Naming one line as the whole business
 *      understates it.
 *   2. The accumulation cycle is mod-50, and the convert-up destination is a
 *      sealed 50g bar. The old copy said "accumulate to 100g, and convert to a
 *      sealed 50g bar" — internally contradictory, and it contradicted our own
 *      homepage, which says 50g in all four places it makes the claim. 100g is
 *      a separate SKU in the standard-bar line, not the accumulation target.
 *   3. "at spot" attaches to the 50g convert-up only. It is accurate there —
 *      `src/app/page.tsx` makes the same promise — so keep the two in step; do
 *      not let this sentence promise spot on anything else.
 *
 * ▶ Length: keep the description at or under ~155 characters. Google truncates
 * a meta description around 155-160, and WhatsApp shows roughly two lines
 * before clipping. `site-metadata.test.ts` pins the ceiling so a future edit
 * cannot silently overflow into an ellipsis mid-sentence.
 */
export const SITE_TITLE = "1Plus1Gold — Every gram brings you one step closer";
 
export const SITE_DESCRIPTION =
  "Singapore's premier gold dealer. Buy Valcambi and PAMP gold from 1g, accumulate gram by gram at falling premiums, and convert to a sealed 50g bar at spot.";
 
/** Ceiling enforced by test — see the length note above. */
export const SITE_DESCRIPTION_MAX_LENGTH = 155;
