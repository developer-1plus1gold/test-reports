/**
 * KYC Expiry Warning Cron Job
 *
 * Runs daily at 04:00 UTC. Sends two pre-expiry WhatsApp notifications:
 *   - 30 days before kyc_expires_at  (informational, plenty of time)
 *   -  1 day  before kyc_expires_at  (last reminder before lockout)
 *
 * Why this exists (2026-05-13 audit gap #5): customers were silently
 * losing their KYC validity and only finding out at checkout when an
 * order > the KYC threshold was rejected. Proactive WhatsApp gives them a runway to
 * renew before they're blocked.
 *
 * Idempotency: profiles.kyc_expiry_warned_30d / _1d flags guard against
 * duplicate sends if the cron runs twice on the same day. Both flags
 * reset to false on the next KYC approval (kyc/webhook/route.ts) so a
 * renewed customer gets warned again at the next expiry cycle.
 *
 * Pluralisation note: the template body says "expires on {{2}}" with
 * the date — no day-count is exposed, so the same template fires for
 * both the 30-day and 1-day tiers. The send-tier distinction lives
 * server-side via the two boolean flags.
 *
 * Protected by CRON_SECRET via verifyCronAuth.
 *
 * ▶ WRITES A HEARTBEAT ON EVERY TICK, INCLUDING THE QUIET ONES. The first
 * warning cannot be due before ~2028, so a silent "sent nothing" is the
 * expected output for years — and is exactly what a dead cron would produce.
 */
