/**
 * The P&L for one window — the single assembly behind the on-screen P&L
 * (`/api/admin/finance/pnl`) and its CSV export (`/api/admin/finance/pnl-csv`).
 *
 * ▶ WHY ONE FUNCTION (2026-10-30). The CSV kept its own copy of this assembly
 * and drifted: it charged conversion-outbound and Gold Yield bars at inventory
 * cost instead of the derived cost_of_conversion / Gold Yield obligation, and
 * it had none of the other derived sets (gateway fees, stock write-offs,
 * equipment depreciation, packing giveaways, amortisation slicing), so the
 * exported profit did not match the screen. Both now call this; there is no
 * second copy to drift.
 *
 * Data sources flowing into the pure aggregator (lib/finance/pnl):
 *   - orders          — completed in the window (revenue recognised at delivery)
 *   - conversions     — paid in the window (revenue: fees)
 *   - sold inventory  — landed-cost COGS; MAG grams via lib/finance/mag-cogs
 *   - accessory lines — the shared detector + landed cost, plus packing giveaways
 *   - ledger_entries  — operating expenses, plus the derived sets above
 *
 * The month-end statements PDF (lib/finance/assemble-financials) calls it too,
 * with explicit windows (month, quarter-to-date, year-to-date). Until
 * 2026-10-30 it kept a third copy that left out the derived gateway fees and
 * kept manually-logged payment_processing_fees rows, so its processing-fee line
 * and net income could differ from the screen (Mukesh: align it with the screen).
 *
 * Throws PnlWindowError when a source cannot be read — never a smaller P&L.
 */
