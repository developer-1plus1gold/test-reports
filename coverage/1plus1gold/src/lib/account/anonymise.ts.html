/**
 * anonymiseAccount — irreversibly scrub ONE account's direct PII in place,
 * keeping FK-linked history (orders / payments / audit trails) de-identified.
 *
 * This is the single source of truth for "make this account forgotten", shared
 * by:
 *   • POST /api/account/delete    — self-service deletion (Apple 5.1.1 + PDPA)
 *   • retention-anonymise-accounts cron — PDPA retention (R1 transacting past
 *     the 5-yr AML window; R2 un-transacted, inactive > 24 months)
 *
 * Extracted 2026-09-07 (PDPA Phase 2+3) so the two callers cannot drift — a
 * scrub that the delete route does but the retention cron forgets (or vice
 * versa) would leave PII behind exactly where we claim it is gone.
 *
 * It ANONYMISES, it does not delete: the customers/profiles/auth rows survive
 * (FK targets for order/payment/audit history) with their identifying fields
 * blanked and every sign-in method removed, so the row can never be resolved
 * back to a person and the account can never be signed into again. One-way; no
 * undelete.
 *
 * Throws at the two hard-failure points (auth tombstone, sign-in-identity
 * purge) so the caller can surface a 500 (delete route) or record + re-raise
 * per account (cron). The earlier best-effort table scrubs mirror the original
 * route's behaviour (a transient miss is re-attempted on the next pass / next
 * cron tick; the identity rows are what actually gate re-entry).
